## Assumptions Presented

### Profile and Origin Resolution

| Assumption | Confidence | Evidence |
|------------|------------|----------|
| Use a versioned, data-only profile registry that keeps app stem, catalog service, and exact admitted origin distinct, with background projecting only the active service/profile into the current tab generation. | Likely | `extension/catalog/recipe-index.generated.js`; `extension/background.js`; `extension/utils/capability-search.js`; `extension/utils/capability-catalog.js` |

**If wrong:** A stem-only or content-inferred lookup could map a service incorrectly, admit a near-neighbor host, leak another app's capabilities into the tab, or require exposing the full catalog to every page.

**Alternatives considered:** resolve a compact bundled registry content-side after background exact-origin admission; inject the complete generated registry into each generation and filter content-side.
