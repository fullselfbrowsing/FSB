## Decisions Made

- Keep the exact admitted origin only in the authority tuple; visible copy never falls back to a raw host, URL, slug, braces, HTML, or profile-supplied component.
- Split ready capability groups into deterministic read, write, and destructive order while keeping every non-ready disposition as a static `status-row`; exactly one ready row receives primary emphasis.
- Treat generic profiles as app-level only even if hostile callers provide forged entity-shaped data, and omit anchor/chip/halo rather than rendering placeholders.
- Revalidate model version, full authority, entity identity, attention policy, copy bounds, and rendered atoms at the shell boundary and immediately before committing a staged rich surface.
- Preserve the existing focus/resource mechanics: Ambient/Anchored do not move host focus, Focused targets its title, Interstitial targets `Keep reviewing`, and backing out restores the exact suspended scope and origin.
