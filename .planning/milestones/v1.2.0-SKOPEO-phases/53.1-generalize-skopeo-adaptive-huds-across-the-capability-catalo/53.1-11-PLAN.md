<source_audit>
- CR-05: A delayed older same-origin SPA route reply can currently reach lifecycle cleanup after a newer reply has installed epoch 3.
- ADAPT-01: Preserve the single projected workflow contract while ensuring its route-derived state is committed only by the current request.
- ADAPT-09: Preserve exact navigation and origin boundaries; newest-wins is local to same-document work and cannot weaken terminal transitions.
- ADAPT-10: Preserve deterministic fail-closed lifecycle behavior without allowing obsolete asynchronous work to become a denial-of-service primitive.
- PATTERNS: Reuse createDeferred race scheduling, per-generation currentness checks, and newest-wins conventions already established in the phase.
- VERIFICATION and REVIEW: Add controlled RED proof, real production harness coverage, and negative controls that fail when the currentness guard is weakened.
</source_audit>

<threat_model>
Standard: OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 Level 1.

- V1 Architecture: Sequence ownership is local to one tab/controller/generation; no global route lock or second executor is introduced.
- V4 Access Control: A reply must prove the exact controller identity and generation captured at dispatch before it may mutate authority-bearing state.
- V5 Validation: Route and epoch data returned asynchronously are untrusted and cannot override locally captured currentness.
- V7 Error Handling: Obsolete work returns a closed non-terminal outcome and cannot be misclassified as a runtime failure requiring cleanup.
- V8 Data Protection: An obsolete route cannot expose, clear, or replace pending arguments, read results, confirmation tokens, or consequence state.
- V11 Business Logic: Newest-first completion, cross-tab spoofing, forged epoch data, kill/replacement invalidation, hard navigation, and origin change are covered explicitly.

Primary abuse cases are stale-route denial of service, cross-tab or cross-generation reply confusion, forged epoch advancement, resurrection after kill, and error classification that terminates a valid newer session.
</threat_model>
