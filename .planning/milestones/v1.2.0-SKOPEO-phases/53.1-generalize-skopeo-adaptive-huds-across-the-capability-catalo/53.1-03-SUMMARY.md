## Decisions Made

- Keep the current raw URL inside the frozen adapter input only; recognized results carry the admitted exact origin and never expose raw URLs, paths, page copy, page objects, or executable values.
- Require adapter-returned exact-origin evidence plus the semantic-kind-specific stable identifier (`drive-item-id`, `docs-document-id`, or `opaque-target-key`) before creating an entity or anchor.
- Treat adapter exceptions and malformed or replayed adapter results as concise retryable uncertainty, while projection/origin/disposal failures are unsupported and non-retryable.
- Use the loaded generated index as an additional profile-tuple consistency check when available, without making the content resolver depend on the full catalog at runtime.
