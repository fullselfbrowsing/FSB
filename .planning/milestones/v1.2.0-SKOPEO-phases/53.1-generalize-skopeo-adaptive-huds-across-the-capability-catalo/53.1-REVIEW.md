## Findings

### BL-01: Normalized result text is not rescanned at the sensitive-data boundary

**Classification:** BLOCKER — router-controlled result material can reach the HUD after evading the only sensitive-text check
**Files:** `extension/background.js` (`safeReadBoundaryText`, `closedReadData`, `safeReadText`, `typedReadResult`); `tests/skopeo-catalog-runtime.test.js`

**Issue:** `safeReadBoundaryText` applies `READ_SECRET_VALUE` to the original string, then removes control and bidirectional formatting characters, sanitizes addresses, compacts whitespace, and returns the normalized string. It never reapplies the sensitive-value policy to that normalized candidate. A prohibited marker split by a character removed during normalization therefore passes the first check; the normalized marker and its associated value are then included in a success notice or structured scalar result. A production-controller negative probe confirmed that marker bytes crossed into the content response and UI-bound typed result.

This affects both paths that rely on the helper: structured values admitted through `closedReadData` and success messages admitted through `safeReadText`. Renderer validation cannot repair the disclosure because it validates the atom shape after the text has already crossed the security boundary.

**Fix:** Build the normalized candidate first and apply the sensitive-value policy to that candidate before returning it. Prefer rejecting disallowed formatting/control characters outright, or rescan after normalization while retaining the existing address and length checks. Add production-controller regressions for prohibited markers divided by removed control, bidirectional, and zero-width formatting characters in both structured values and success messages; require the fixed completion notice and prove none of the marker bytes cross into content.
