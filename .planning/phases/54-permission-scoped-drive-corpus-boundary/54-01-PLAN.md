<threat_model>
## Trust boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Drive/account claims → durable keys | Attacker-controlled IDs can collide, omit tuple members, or substitute another account/root. |
| Remote metadata → local records | Unknown fields, prototypes, raw payloads, or oversized strings can cross into trusted state. |
| Change evidence → source state/fingerprint | A rename, opaque 404, or transient failure can be misclassified as content change or authoritative deletion. |

## STRIDE register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Disposition | Mitigation and automated negative proof |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| T-54-04 | High | Spoofing / Tampering | mitigate | Exact versioned tuple/source key constructors validate every component and reject missing, extra, ambiguous, legacy-global, and cross-partition forms; schema tests attempt each substitution. |
| T-54-05 | High | Information disclosure | mitigate | Visibility and six source states are closed; `pending` and `inaccessible` records cannot carry display/source-derived fields, while `missing` requires authoritative reconciliation evidence. Negative tests try stale names, snippets, counts, and citations. |
| T-54-07 | High | Tampering | mitigate | Closed plain-object parsing rejects unknown keys, accessors, prototypes, raw errors, HTML, and unbounded remote strings. Hostile-object tests prove rejection without getter execution. |
| T-54-10 | High | Tampering / Repudiation | mitigate | Metadata, membership, and content fingerprints have distinct schemas; content identity accepts exact revision/checksum/export-byte-hash evidence only. Tests prove rename/move cannot alter the content fingerprint. |
| T-54-09 | Medium | Denial of service | mitigate | Every collection/string/depth is capped and canonicalization is cycle-safe. Tests cover cycles, deep nesting, and over-limit inputs. |
</threat_model>
