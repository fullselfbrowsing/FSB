<threat_model>
## Trust boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Exact corpus schema → Chrome local storage | Multi-key writes are not transactional and can stop at any await or quota error. |
| Active partition → replacement/account switch | Two corpora or an old account may be briefly visible together. |
| Source state → downstream owned categories | Tombstoned source facts may remain in future indexes/citations/caches/alerts. |
| Worker wake → persisted journal/manifest | Partial operations can replay out of order or open before account proof. |

## STRIDE register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Disposition | Mitigation and automated negative proof |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| T-54-04 | High | Spoofing / Tampering | mitigate | Every API requires and reparses the full versioned tuple/source key; storage values are cross-checked against their keys. Tests swap accounts, roots, source IDs, and record bodies. |
| T-54-06 | High | Tampering / Information disclosure | mitigate | A single closed manifest points to visibility; tombstone/withdrawal is persisted first, participants purge and prove absence second, checkpoints/pointers publish last. Failure is injected before/after every storage and participant await. |
| T-54-05 | High | Information disclosure | mitigate | Withheld/purging records are omitted from visible reads immediately and source-owned categories must report zero before terminal completion. Tests seed all fake categories and stop/recover at every boundary. |
| T-54-03 | High | Elevation of privilege | mitigate | Operation certificates are never accepted or persisted by the store; authority must re-prove externally. Tests attempt certificate/replay fields and inspect raw storage. |
| T-54-09 | Medium | Denial of service | mitigate | Per-partition lanes, bounded journals/records/participants, idempotent operation IDs, and retryable closed recovery prevent unbounded queues or duplicate work. Tests flood, duplicate, reorder, and quota-fail operations. |
</threat_model>
