<interfaces>
Closed operation kinds are exactly:
```text
ingestion | query | display | citation-open | alert-delivery
```
Authority exposes `beginOperation(kind, liveContext)`, `certifySource(operation, sourceFileId)`, `certifySources(operation, exactSourceFileIds)`, `runWithCertifiedSource(operation, sourceFileId, callback)`, `runWithCertifiedSources(operation, exactSourceFileIds, callback)`, and `finishOperation(operation)`. Each call requires one exact source ID or a nonempty deduplicated source-ID set under a named maximum; no implicit all/current-source fallback or partition scan is accepted. Certificates are frozen in-memory capabilities bound to operation ID/kind, tab/origin/generation/context epoch, authority epoch, account permission ID, root/source IDs, partition/source epochs, proof timestamps, and decision. They are never serializable or accepted as caller data. For `display`, the trusted callback receives only successfully certified exact sources and may assemble their individual rows; an aggregate is allowed only if the entire originally requested set certified and remains current. The authority repeats full tuple/source currentness for every included/requested source after assembly, discarding affected rows and the whole aggregate on mismatch.
</interfaces>
