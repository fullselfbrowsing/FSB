<interfaces>
Expose a background-only injected transport with six fixed operations:
```text
about()
getFile({ fileId, resourceKey? })
listChildren({ parentFileId, pageToken?, driveId? })
getStartPageToken({ driveId? })
listChanges({ pageToken, driveId? })
readContent({ fileId, mimeType, resourceKey? }, operationSink)
```
The corresponding in-page actions live under a literal private namespace such as `skopeo-drive-corpus` and accept exact parameter schemas. They are not capabilities, are absent from generated/public catalogs, and cannot accept an arbitrary endpoint/request descriptor.

The v1 read policy is literal, not family-based:
```text
application/vnd.google-apps.document -> files.export(mimeType="text/plain")
text/plain                            -> files.get(alt="media")
MAX_EXACT_BYTES                       = 10_485_760
```
PDF, Sheets, Slides, binary, HTML, Markdown, CSV, and every other/generic `text/*` MIME are unsupported/unreadable. A declared or streamed byte 10,485,761 rejects/cancels the entire read; no truncation, partial decode, or partial hash is allowed. `canDownload:false` or a content-specific denial maps to download-blocked. Unknown authenticated response variants are unsupported until fixture-locked.
</interfaces>

<threat_model>
## Trust boundaries

| Boundary | Risk |
|---|---|
| Background operation → page-owned authenticated `gapi` | A forged origin/action/request can spend the user's Drive authority or expose unrelated files. |
| Provider response/error → trusted authority/reconciler | Collapsed 403/404, malicious strings, or extra fields can misclassify access and leak provider data. |
| Source download/export → operation-local consumer | Oversized/partial/unsupported bytes or decoded text can escape into storage/log/UI. |
| Change/list pagination → inventory | Untrusted tokens, incomplete search, or shortcut targets can broaden membership. |

## STRIDE register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Disposition | Mitigation and automated negative proof |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| T-54-01 | High | Spoofing / Elevation of privilege | mitigate | Require an exact allowed Drive/Docs origin and one of six literal actions; bind execution through existing page-read origin checks. Tests forge origins, tabs, namespaces, methods, endpoints, fields, and actions and prove zero page calls. |
| T-54-02 | High | Elevation of privilege | mitigate | Preserve physical `parents`, `driveId`, and `shortcutDetails` only; never fetch/follow shortcut targets. Accept `resourceKey` only from validated exact-source trusted metadata and never from content/cross-source claims. Use `supportsAllDrives`/`includeItemsFromAllDrives` exact request shapes. Tests attempt shortcut, shared-drive, and resource-key broadening. |
| T-54-05 | High | Information disclosure | mitigate | Normalize status/code into closed typed results, retain no raw body/message/stack, classify 403 and opaque 404 distinctly, and allowlist response fields/caps. Malicious provider fixtures inspect outputs/log sinks. |
| T-54-09 | High | Denial of service / Tampering | mitigate | Bound pages/items/tokens/ancestry-facing metadata and exact source bytes; accept only the two literal MIME paths; reject/cancel at byte 10,485,761, incomplete/cyclic tokens, truncation, malformed UTF-8, and partial hashes. Tests cover 10,485,760 success and 10,485,761 failure for declared and streamed bodies. |
| T-54-10 | High | Tampering / Repudiation | mitigate | Hash exact downloaded/exported bytes before decoding and expose content fingerprint separately from metadata/version. Tests mutate rename/version/bytes independently. |
</threat_model>
