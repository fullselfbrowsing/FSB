## Required observation notes

For every genre row, record whether explicit invocation alone started Skopeo; whether the exact app/profile and app-native nouns were correct; whether the first state was Ambient or truthfully Anchored; whether non-ready actions remained non-interactive; and whether Focused or Interstitial appeared only after the corresponding explicit Skopeo control.

The accessibility observation must cover names, roles, safe-first alert-dialog order, focus entry/restoration, one live region, 200% zoom, a viewport below 480 CSS pixels, reduced motion, and VoiceOver. The host-coexistence observation must exercise scrolling, menus or editors, virtualized/reused content where available, and confirm no host mutation or blocked control. The teardown observation must cover turn-off, hard navigation, same-origin SPA replacement, origin change, and no residual shell, focus target, announcement, or action completion.
