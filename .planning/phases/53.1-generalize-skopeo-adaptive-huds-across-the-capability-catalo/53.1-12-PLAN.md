<threat_model>
Standard: OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 Level 1.

- V1 Architecture: The test proves one generator-to-router authority chain and rejects alternate or direct executors.
- V4 Access Control: Compare only intended authority relations: resolved.origin equals executionOrigin, executionOrigin is admitted, and interactive current-page origin equals executionOrigin. Service identity is not execution authority.
- V5 Validation: Schemas and arguments are untrusted. Reject extra and prototype-polluting keys, wrong types, non-finite numbers, oversized values, and missing required secrets before invocation.
- V6 Cryptography: The canonical full-schema digest binds projected intent to background-resolved authority; changing a hidden thirteenth field while retaining the display summary must fail.
- V7 Error Handling: Unsupported schemas, stale tokens, obsolete routes, and validation failures fail closed without side effects or misleading success evidence.
- V8 Data Protection: Full schemas and secret values remain background-only; projections expose only the digest and bounded non-secret paramSummary.
- V9 Communications: Execution origin comes from trusted installed resolver data, never projected or user-supplied origin strings.
- V11 Business Logic: Target spoofing, cancel, forged token, digest mismatch, replay, concurrent confirmation, double invocation, stale route completion, and kill resurrection all have explicit zero-invocation or zero-mutation assertions.

The closure ledger is evidence, not authority: automated success must not set, imply, or rewrite live_approved in HUMAN-UAT.md.
</threat_model>
