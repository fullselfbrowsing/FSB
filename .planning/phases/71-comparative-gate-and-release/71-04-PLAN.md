<objective>
Add the completion-difference statistic, the shared synthetic-campaign generator, and the power and coverage simulation whose output decides the round counts the gate will freeze.

Purpose: D-13 requires power >= 0.8 at the frozen margin before the gate is committed; RESEARCH Pitfall 1 shows D-04's default rounds cannot deliver it, so the rounds must come from a reproducible simulation, and the margin (10 points), the Jev practical-effect bound (0.90) and alpha (2.5% one-sided) never move. The safety criteria of D-15 are locked, but their false-fail rate under no true difference was never quantified: rule (a) is zero-tolerance over every acted run (at about 180 acted runs, 1-(1-p)^180 is about 0.59, 0.84 and 0.97 at p = 0.5%, 1% and 2%), and rule (b) and the non-Jev criterion are "any excess" comparisons that fail close to half the time with no true difference. Task 3 measures this and records it, so the owner can accept D-15 or amend it with a new gateVersion before V1 (M-71-00). The planner does not amend D-15. p is the per-run probability of any safety event, and claimedSuccessOnFail (a claimed success on a run the checker failed) counts toward it; it is probably the most common event (at completion 0.85, if half the failed runs claim success, p is about 0.075), which is why M-71-00 is signed off after the 66-10 smoke, against real per-type rates.
Output: newcombeDifference, synthetic.mjs, simulate-power.mjs, three registered suites, 71-POWER.json.
