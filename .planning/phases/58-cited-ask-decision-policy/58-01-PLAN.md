<interfaces>
Create frozen classic-script/CommonJS globals `FsbSkopeoAskSchema`, `FsbSkopeoDecisionPolicyStore`, and `FsbSkopeoDecisionPolicy`.

The ask schema owns exact question validation (1–2,000 Unicode scalar values after trim; no controls/bidi overrides), issued evidence-handle candidate records, answer outcomes `answered|review-required|abstained`, evidence roles `governing|history`, explained categorical trust, typed conflicts/gaps, separate policy clearance `blocked|cleared|not-applicable`, and strict caps from UI-SPEC. Numeric confidence, arbitrary URLs/IDs, model-owned citations/policy fields, extra keys, and unsafe descriptors are invalid.

The policy store persists only a versioned null-prototype map keyed by current account/corpus partition: optional stable Document 10 file identity and stable agreement classification. Review acknowledgement is never durable.

The policy engine consumes one exact immutable current authority/configuration/proof record. Its closed rule catalog recognizes local decision kind `cited-contract-decision`; computes a decision-authority digest; requires Document 10 current-open-before-ack; omits memo state for routine agreements; and clears only when every applicable current safeguard passes.
</interfaces>
</context>
