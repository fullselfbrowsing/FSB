<objective>
Make session recording safe and useful for the settings routes: never persist a submitted value, record compact summaries, keep the write facts in the journal, and prove with a sentinel that a refused secret leaves no trace anywhere.

Purpose: SC2 requires that refused values never reach storage, journal, logs or error text. The recorder's key-name redaction misses `{key:'apiKey', value:'sk-...'}` and `serverHashKey` (D-19), and the journal's action compaction would drop key/previous/value (Pitfall 4).
Output: two sanitizers in the dispatcher, two journal-table entries in the recorder, and new cases in `tests/mcp-settings-route.test.js`.
</objective>
